Bringing up my first 4-layer board

2026-10-12 · PCB

The boards arrived on a Tuesday. The temptation is always to plug in USB and see what happens. This time I followed a checklist instead, and it caught a reversed footprint before anything let out the magic smoke.

Before power

  • Inspect under magnification: solder bridges, tombstoned passives, IC orientation.
  • Measure every rail to ground with a multimeter. Anything under a few ohms needs explaining.
  • Check the input protection with the diode-test mode.

First power

Use a bench supply with the current limit set just above the expected idle draw, then bring the voltage up slowly while watching the current. 

Rail    Expected   Measured   Idle current
VIN     12.0 V     12.01 V    —
5V0     5.00 V     4.98 V     18 mA
3V3     3.30 V     3.29 V     6 mA

What I'd do differently

Add test points to every rail and a 0 Ω jumper after each regulator, so each stage can be isolated without lifting parts.
