Instrumentation Amp Front-End

Analog · 2025

A low-noise bridge-sensor front-end with a 4th-order anti-aliasing filter feeding a 24-bit ΔΣ ADC. Measured noise is 0.6 µV RMS.

Overview

The front-end reads a load-cell bridge. Most of the work went into the noise budget: resistor thermal noise, amplifier voltage and current noise, and reference noise.

A 4th-order Butterworth anti-aliasing filter sits between the in-amp and the ADC driver.

Gain128 V/V
Input noise0.6 µV RMS (0.1–10 Hz)
Filter4th-order Butterworth, 1 kHz
ADC24-bit ΔΣ
ToolsLTspice, Python
