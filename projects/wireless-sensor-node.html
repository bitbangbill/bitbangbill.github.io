Wireless Sensor Node

PCB · Embedded · 2026

Low-power 4-layer board built around an ESP32-C3 with an environmental sensor suite. It sleeps at 8 µA and runs over a year on a single 18650.

Overview

The goal was a sensor node I could leave in the garden for a year without touching it. Everything else followed from the power budget: the radio is off except for a short burst every five minutes, and every rail can be switched off.

The board is 4 layers with a solid ground plane under the RF section. Firmware is ESP-IDF with deep sleep between readings.

MCUESP32-C3
Layers4
Sleep current8 µA
Battery1× 18650 Li-ion
SensorsBME280, SHT40, ambient light
ToolsKiCad, ESP-IDF
